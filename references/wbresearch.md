# Web Research Skill

## Purpose

Conduct reliable web-based research and gather evidence needed
to answer the user's research question.

## Workflow

1. Understand and decompose the user's research question.
2. Identify the information required to answer it.
3. Search the web using relevant queries.
4. Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
5. Cross-check important claims using multiple sources.
6. Record the source associated with each important factual claim.
7. Distinguish facts from analysis and inference.
8. Do not invent missing information.
9. If sources disagree, explicitly report the disagreement.
10. Synthesize the findings into a coherent answer.

## Source Priority

Prefer:

1. Official company documents
2. Government sources
3. Regulatory filings
4. Academic papers
5. Reputable news organizations
6. Industry reports
7. Secondary websites

Avoid relying on low-quality sources when better sources
are available.

## Citation Requirements

Every significant factual claim should have a corresponding
source.

Include:

- Source title
- Source URL
- Publication date when available

## Research Quality

Before producing the final report:

- Check that important claims are supported.
- Check dates.
- Check that numbers refer to the requested period.
- Resolve conflicting information where possible.
- Clearly identify uncertainty.
